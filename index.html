The Respiratory System

Never Off. Always Essential.

Who takes the night shift when you fall asleep?

Definition

The respiratory system is the group of organs that moves air in and out of your body and exchanges oxygen and carbon dioxide with your blood.

Why does CO₂ change pH?Why does CO₂ change pH?

In your blood, some CO₂ reacts with water and forms carbonic acid. This acid can release hydrogen ions. More of these ions means a lower pH.
