O₂

Fuel your cells.

Oxygen intake

Oxygen diffuses from air in the alveoli into the blood. The blood carries oxygen to your cells for cellular respiration.

CO₂

Take out the waste.

Carbon dioxide removal

Cells make carbon dioxide as they release energy from food. Blood carries it to your lungs, and you breathe it out.

pH

Keep the balance.

Blood pH regulation

By adjusting how much carbon dioxide you exhale, your respiratory system helps keep blood pH steady.

Homeostasis in action

Your body breathes. Then it adjusts.

  1. CO₂ rises

    Cells release extra carbon dioxide. Blood becomes more acidic and pH falls.

  2. Breathing adjusts

    Sensors detect changes linked to CO₂. The brain signals faster or deeper breathing.

  3. More CO₂ leaves

    Faster or deeper breathing removes more CO₂.

  4. pH recovers

    As CO₂ falls, blood pH returns toward its starting level.

Why it matters. Stable blood pH helps cells and enzymes work properly. That’s one way breathing helps keep conditions inside your body steady.
